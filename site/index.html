Many ways to keep things, one app

Every mode works on the same folders, so what you write once can be seen the way that suits the moment.

Canvas

Notes, pictures, clocks and timers placed freely on a board you arrange.

Pages

Write anywhere on a page, put blocks side by side, and link pages and folders together.

Tasks and habits

Lists with goals of time or times, and habits with streaks, schedules and a year of days.

Calendar

Plan your day or your week in colour, right next to your notes.

Your music

Play your own tracks and playlists while you work. They stay on your device.

Themes and wallpapers

Dozens of themes, see-through ones included, and a wallpaper for every folder.

Everywhere you are

Sign in and your folders follow you between your computer and your phone, even after working offline.

Yours to take

Export a folder or everything, pictures included, whenever you like.

On your phone

Made for your phone too

The same folders and the same look, with the controls where your thumb is.
